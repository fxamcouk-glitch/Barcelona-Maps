    ✈︎ Barcelona Airport (BCN)

    Getting there

    El Prat has two terminals, about 4 km apart, with a free shuttle bus between them. Check your boarding pass for T1 or T2.

    Easiest
    Aerobús from either terminal to Plaça Catalunya (about 35 minutes, every few minutes; pay on board by card or online). Then two stops on the L3 to Diagonal, or a 20-minute walk up Passeig de Gràcia.
    Cheaper
    Train R2 Nord from T2 to Passeig de Gràcia, about 25 minutes, then one stop on the L3 or L5 to Diagonal. From T1, take the shuttle to T2 first.
    Taxi
    About 25–30 minutes from the rank outside arrivals.

    Passports & documents

    This phone only

    Photos of passports, GHIC cards or travel insurance, kept as a backup in case the originals go missing. These are saved only on this phone. They're never uploaded or shared, and they open with no signal. Each of you adds your own on your own phone.

    If you lose your passport abroad, contact the nearest British Consulate. Barcelona has one, and a photo of the photo page speeds things up.
